Run Analysis will not invent a scenario.

Forecast and scenario routes are disabled because ercot-delta-quantile-v1.4 failed its predeclared calibration gate at Q90, Q95, and Q99. Absolute coverage error had to be ≤ 0.03. Observed errors were 0.035417, 0.04375, and 0.031667.

Why this button does not run a model

There is no validated production artifact on this deployment, and no weather slider is offered as a stand-in. A 503 from /api/gert/scenario means the capability is off. It does not mean a placeholder forecast is waiting behind the control.

The calculation that does run is the Historical Lab: published hourly load, an assumed capacity, and a uniform reduction, with every hour downloadable from the checksummed archive.
