Thresholds were set first. Then the model failed them.

Dresden Goehner froze the promotion gates for ercot-delta-quantile-v1.4 before the evaluation window was opened. On the untouched 96-hour window the candidate missed Q90, Q95, and Q99. That rejection, published 2026-08-30, is the front of this site. The Historical Lab below can be recomputed from a public archive.

Official ERCOT load and live weather are not connected on gertgrid.space. No estimated megawatt or weather value is shown in their place. Prediction and scenario endpoints stay closed.

Reproducible Historical Lab

216 published hours. Your assumptions. No forecast.

Three 72-hour windows of ERCOT hourly native load, from dataset DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22975251. Capacity and the uniform reduction are assumptions. Gap energy is the sum of hourly shortfalls against that assumption. It is not expected unserved energy, and it is not the rejected probabilistic model.

Reference figures below are for summer 2023 at 80,000 MW and a 5% reduction. The archive checksum is checked before any figure is replaced.

Grid Extreme Risk Toolkit (GERT) is decision-support research. It is not an autonomous grid controller, and this deployment does not show live ERCOT load.
