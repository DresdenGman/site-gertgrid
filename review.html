What stayed gated

Production prediction, scenario analysis, and validated backtest stay unavailable. This site returns 503 for those routes and does not invent a forecast. The same evidence file is served at /api/gert/model/evidence and stored in the main repository as evidence/ercot_v1_4_validation.json.

Limits of this record

  • 96 hourly observations are a small sample for tail quantiles.
  • Positive pinball skill is not calibration.
  • GERT is decision-support research and must not be used for autonomous grid control.
  • Data provenance for the candidate: official ERCOT system-load archives joined with ERA5 weather. That is the evaluation record, not a live feed on this website.

How to critique the evidence.

A review is a check you can repeat: open a historical case, recompute one row, and say what you found. It is not a model validation and it does not authorize a forecast.

Criticism is recorded only if you submit it. The structured GitHub form and the public discussion are the record. The community page lists what people have published and which notes are still open. Private replies stay off that page. A comment is not an endorsement.

An introductory teaching activity, not a completed review of the historical dataset.

One complete review, about 10 minutes.

  1. Open the summer case at 80,000 MW and 0% reduction. Note the hours above assumed capacity and the gap energy.
  2. Switch to 5% reduction. Explain why the hours and energy change. Can you distinguish the actual observations from the two assumptions?
  3. Download the JSON report and hourly CSV. Recompute one row as max(0, adjusted load − capacity). Sum the hourly gaps to check the total in MWh.
  4. Inspect the winter case. Why would measured load during outages be insufficient to reconstruct unmet demand?
  5. Share one result you checked and one change you would make. A negative finding is useful.

What this release establishes

Three public-data cases, reproducible deterministic calculations, downloadable reports, and an open review process. User-defined capacity is not historical capacity; counterfactual gap energy is not expected unserved energy. The probabilistic research candidate remains gated until its validation requirements pass.

Grid Extreme Risk Toolkit (GERT) is decision-support research. It is not an autonomous grid controller, and this deployment does not show live ERCOT load.
