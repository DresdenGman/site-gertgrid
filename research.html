The claim is that the miss stays inspectable.

Grid Extreme Risk Toolkit (GERT) is public research software. A candidate with positive pinball skill was rejected because it missed gates that were fixed in advance. That is the result this site leads with.

Probabilistic prediction stays closed. This page is the rejection record, the Historical Lab, and the PJM benchmark.

Sister benchmark · negative result

PJM cold-spell calibration did not transfer.

PJM-Cold-Calibration-Benchmark (10.5281/zenodo.24010850, v1.0.0, published 2026-11-06; repository DresdenGman/PJM-Cold-Calibration-Benchmark) froze its protocol before computing results. The headline is a failure, not a tuning success.

0 of 18

Horizon × learner cells. No calibrator selected without the target event restored event-window coverage to nominal.

The repository also records coverage gaps of +14.3 percentage points for the 2017–18 snap and +9.2 percentage points for Winter Storm Elliott on that same day-ahead QR-GBT arm. Those are separate events. The strict pre-registered form of the collapse judgement narrowly missed on Elliott.
